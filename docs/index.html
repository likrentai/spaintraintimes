How to read it

Distances are exact only from the centre city: on the map, two other cities look closer than the train really makes them, because most trips between them still go through Madrid. The coastline is a backdrop, stretched between the cities; only the cities are measured. The faint line is the real coast. Rings mark each hour from the centre.

In 2025 and 2026, Compare with car adds a grey mark on each city's line at its driving time on today's roads: a dot inside its mark is quicker by train. Dots use a simplified network of key links (60 min per change of train); the info card gives the fastest trip in the timetable, so in a few views away from Madrid a dot can sit on the wrong side of its mark.
